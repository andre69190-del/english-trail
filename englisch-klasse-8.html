Themen und Wortschatz

🏘️ Stadt und Land
Ich kann Leben in der Stadt und auf dem Land vergleichen und Vermutungen mit if-Sätzen (Typ II) äußern.
Beispielwörter: countryside, city life, rural, urban, neighbourhood, public transport, traffic, pollution …
🌍 Natur und Umwelt
Ich kann über Umweltprobleme sprechen und mit dem Passiv beschreiben, was getan wird.
Beispielwörter: environment, climate change, to recycle, waste, plastic, energy, to save, to protect …
🗽 Die USA
Ich kann Grundlegendes über die USA erzählen und wiedergeben, was andere gesagt haben (indirekte Rede).
Beispielwörter: state, president, flag, coast to coast, skyscraper, national park, high school, grade …
🏰 Britische Geschichte
Ich kann über wichtige Ereignisse der britischen Geschichte berichten (simple past, passive).
Beispielwörter: history, century, king, queen, castle, battle, to rule, to invade …
💚 Wohlbefinden und Stress
Ich kann über Stress und Gefühle sprechen und Freunden Tipps geben.
Beispielwörter: stress, stressed, nervous, worried, to relax, to calm down, to take a break, to talk to someone …

Grammatik in Klasse 8

  • Past progressive – I was watching TV when the phone rang.
  • Present perfect oder simple past – I went to Paris last year.
  • If-Satz Typ I – If it rains, we will stay at home.
  • Relativsätze (who / which / whose) – The girl who lives next door is my friend.
  • Adjektiv oder Adverb – She sings very beautifully.
  • If-Satz Typ II – If I were rich, I would buy a castle.
  • Past perfect – When we arrived, the film had already started.
  • Passiv – English is spoken all over the world.
  • Indirekte Rede – Tom: "I am tired." – Tom said that he was tired.
  • Present perfect progressive – I have been waiting for you for an hour!
  • Indirekte Rede (ohne Zeitverschiebung) – Anna: "I like cats." → Anna says that she likes cats.
  • Artikel: a, an, the oder ohne – I'd like an apple, please.
  • Question tags – You're from Berlin, aren't you?
  • Verbindungswörter (although, because, so that …) – Although it was raining, we went for a walk.
  • Past perfect progressive – She was tired because she had been working all day.

Zum Wiederholen: Simple present, Present progressive, can / must / may, Simple past, going to-future, will-future, Present perfect, Steigerung (comparison), some / any, much / many, Possessivbegleiter und Pronomen, s-Genitiv (Tom's, the girls'), Fragewörter und Kurzantworten, Häufigkeitsadverbien (always, never …), have to / needn't / mustn't.
